## AI resoruces for teaching

- [**Quizzer**](https://apps.ai-in-edu.energy.aau.dk/report-commenter) is a tool that helps students practice course material. You as a supervisor provide course material and questions. Then the students can talk to an AI about the questions and receive personal feedback and suggestions for missing knowledge.

- **API Access to LLM's** may be helpful if you plan to include AI as part of your teaching. We offer API access to some of the strongest models currently available. Currently, we host the latest [**`DeepSeek-V4-Flash`**](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731) model.

## AI resoruces for supervision

- [**Report commenter**](https://apps.ai-in-edu.energy.aau.dk/report-commenter) is a tool that helps you to focus supervisor meetings on useful discussions rather than formalities. Using this tool, you may login and supply your own supervision style (in the form of prompts). Then the students may upload their student report and get detailed feedback.
