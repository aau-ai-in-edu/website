## AI resoruces for teaching

- [**Quizzer**](https://apps.ai-in-edu.energy.aau.dk/report-commenter) is a tool that helps students practice course material. You as a supervisor provide course material such as slides, books, study curriculum. Next, you provide questions; either directly or get AI to draft them. When students sign in and choose your name from the drop-down list, they will be asked those questions along with AI-generated follow-up questions depending on their answers. When the session ends the students will receive personal recommondations for what and how to improve their knowledge level.

- **API Access to LLMs** may be helpful if you plan to include AI as part of your teaching. We offer API access to some of the strongest models currently available. Currently, we host the latest [**`DeepSeek-V4-Flash`**](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731) model.

## AI resoruces for supervision

- [**Report commenter**](https://apps.ai-in-edu.energy.aau.dk/report-commenter) is a tool that helps students get immediate feedback on written material. Students upload a PDF file and receive feedback in minutes. To ensure the comments are truly useful, you as a supervisor may login and provide detailed instructions for the AI. When the students sign in they may choose you from a list of supervisors, who provided detailed instructions. Please get in touch to help get started with adding your own commenting instructions. *Note:* this tool is only intended to free up time for more important discussions and should not replace proper supervision.
